<section class="academic-section" id="about-me" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
  <h2 id="about-heading">About Me</h2>
  <p>I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at <a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/">Imperial College London</a>, supervised by Professor <a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/p.dragotti">Pier Luigi Dragotti</a>. My research focuses on computer vision, signal processing, and deep learning for inverse problems, with an emphasis on image restoration, diffusion models, and compressive sensing.</p>
  <p>I received my bachelor's degree in electronic and information engineering from Dalian University of Technology in 2019 and my master's degree in computer applications technology from Peking University, Shenzhen, in 2022. During my master's studies, I was supervised by Professor <a href="https://jianzhang.tech/">Jian Zhang</a>. I was awarded the President's Ph.D. Scholarship by Imperial College London.</p>
</section>
